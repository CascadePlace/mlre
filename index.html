SHEET 02 / 06

The book

Stanley Richards works at Bolt Robotics, the most powerful engineering company in the world. His father runs it. He is also, according to his manager, “the worst engineer I have ever worked with in my career.”

His robots work… mostly. They also cut pizza with a machete, fire parcels straight through front doors and force cupcakes on anyone in reach. Every week, Chloe Summers arrives with another disaster report, and every week Stanley promises to be more practical.

What nobody at Bolt Robotics knows is where all his time is really going. Under a canvas sheet in his workshop sits an unfinished robolt with a blank screen for a face: a Bolt Buddy, built not for work but for friendship. Stanley believes it could make him his first real friend, earn his father’s respect and maybe even help humans and robots trust each other again.

But it’s been eight years since the war between robots and humans ended, and the people of Blackwire haven’t forgotten. How far will Stanley go to build the perfect robot buddy?

SHEET 03 / 06

Welcome to Blackwire

Technical notes on the world of the book. No spoilers past chapter two.

LOC-01

Wattsville, Blackwire · 2046

A sunny, bustling city where AI is built into almost everything, from the kitchen to the living room. It’s a subscription society: people own nothing and rent everything, houses included.

ORG-01

Bolt Robotics

Founded by the late John Richards and now run by his son Barron. It’s the envy of the engineering world, and nearly everything made in and out of Blackwire passes through its factories and conveyor belts.

HIST-01

The Blackwire Incident

A three-year war between humans and AI-controlled robots ended nearly eight years ago. It never spread beyond the city, but it still hangs over everyone who lives there.

SYS-01

Cyrus pipes: “Shoot To Send”

Huge pipes built into the walls send robots and parts across Bolt Robotics and out through Blackwire. People joke that “STS” sounds like a disease. One pipe runs all the way to a high-security AI facility.

R&D-01

Generation Gamma

Four processors fused into one glowing green square: “Gen G”, the brain module meant to give every robot and robolt a near-human personality. It’s top secret and very expensive. Mass production starts next month.

SEC-01

AI facilities

There are fifty-one worldwide, holding information never meant for the public. Only one has ever been broken into. The intruders lasted four minutes.

SHEET 04 / 06

Personnel files

Bolt Robotics staff directory. Internal use only. (Please do not show Mr Richards his file.)

EMP-0001-SRUnder review

Stanley Richards

Junior engineer · son of the CEO

A self-described jack of all trades, which in practice means he’s about half as good as the people who are good at things. He’s a capable engineer with big, wildly impractical ideas, and he is terrible at coding. He loves his job and burgers and ice cream, and he would really, really like a friend.

“What if robots could be more than just coworkers?”
MGR-0114-CSManager

Chloe Summers

Stanley’s manager

By the book, sharp and fiercely professional. She files Stanley’s weekly disaster reports and has spent years trying to rein him in. She’ll correct you if you call her Chloe.

“That is Ms Summers to you.”
EMP-0388-SGNew hire

Serena Grimm

Junior engineer · three weeks in

She works hard, keeps a blank expression at all times and spends a long time choosing at the food service bot. She can’t quite name what she feels, but she knows the vending bot is out of Boss Coffee, and she notices everything.

“…This is very confusing.”
MGR-0072-MCManager

Matthew “Matt” Crankshaft

Serena’s manager

Patient, mostly. He has a headache most days, and his equipment keeps mysteriously going missing.

EXE-0002-ENExecutive

Edward Nixon

Chief Operating Officer

He wears a dark green suit traced with circuitry and a bright blue bowtie. He runs the company day to day while the CEO is away, and he has strong opinions on spirit. (Stanley isn’t convinced he exists.)

“Spirit! It’s second only to electricity powering life itself!”
EXE-0001-BRClassified

Barron Richards

CEO · “The Metal Baron”

A war hero said to be strong enough to tear the limbs off a bear. He’s rarely seen at the office, busy with politics and private business. He is Stanley’s father, and the man Stanley most wants to make proud.

SCI-0040-OOR&D

Oman Obonno

Lead lab scientist

He knows a lot of company secrets and spends his spare time annoying coworkers. He wanted normal potatoes.

“That burger had a family!”
PRJ-BB-01Unregistered

Boltley

Bolt Buddy · Stanley’s side project

A small robolt with an LED face, bright rectangular eyes and a smile. Stanley built him for friendship, not work, and named him after an hour of deliberation. (The other option was Greg.) His memory storage is 1 MB. He needs work.

“Hello. I am registered as your Buddy.”

Also on the payroll: Bob the janitor, a former AI programmer who lets the robot do the mopping; Ryan, who really, really likes pizza; and a Cupcake Bot you should never leave alone in a dusty room.

My Little Robot Employee is M.S. Picton’s first book. It began as a concept in early 2025 and grew, one bolt at a time, into a full world of robolts, rival factories and one very unlucky engineer.

Volume 2 is already in the works: a wild new adventure for Stanley. Expect more robots, more of Blackwire, and spiritual magic playing a bigger part than you might think.
